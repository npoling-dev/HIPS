Tool Purpose & Institutional Rationale

The TAMU High-Impact Practices (HIPs) Course Profile Tool provides a structured framework for faculty, program directors, and curriculum committees to design, evaluate, and scale high-impact learning experiences. By aligning course activities with AAC&U quality elements and Texas A&M Undergraduate Learning Outcomes (ULOs), this tool ensures pedagogical rigor, institutional fidelity, and verifiable student success across academic and co-curricular programs.
